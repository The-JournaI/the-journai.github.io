Why it matters

Four subjects, one system.

A rent cap is housing policy, a tax on landlords and a bet on how investors will respond. A bond auction is a market event that decides what a government can afford to promise.

Politicians set the rules, businesses and markets react, and the reaction shapes the next election. Follow only one of those and the rest look like surprises. This site tries to follow the whole loop.
