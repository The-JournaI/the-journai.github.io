I'm a young writer who is passionate about politics, economics, finance, and business. I write about everything from why a bond auction goes badly, to what a rent cap really does, to the economics behind businesses.

Together, business, finance, economics, and politics keep turning out to be one subject, so understanding the combination is imperative to operating like an informed citizen of the world. My habit is to price an idea before I defend it, and to say so when I get one wrong. Nothing here is any type of financial or investment advice.
