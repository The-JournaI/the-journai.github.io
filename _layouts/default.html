I'm a young writer with no institution behind me and no editor above me. I write about whatever I can't stop thinking about: why a bond auction goes badly, what a rent cap really does, why a government's arithmetic doesn't close.

Business, finance, economics and politics keep turning out to be one subject. My habit is to price an idea before I defend it, and to say so when I get one wrong. Nothing here is investment advice.
